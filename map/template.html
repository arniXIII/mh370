MH370: where the seabed was searched, and where the debris washed ashore

Three seabed searches along the 7th arc (2014–17, 2018, 2025–26) found nothing. 33 items washed up on the shores of the western Indian Ocean. This is a working map for estimating where probability remains. The 2014–17 coverage is measured data; for 2018 only the outer boundary is known; the 2025–26 coverage is estimated from the ship's track.

How accurate this map is

  • 7th arc: equal slant range from the Inmarsat-3F1 satellite on the WGS84 ellipsoid, at 20,000 ft. The satellite position (0.54°N, 64.46°E) and the range were fitted to the arc published by CAPTION (residual SD 25 m). Check: the UGIB last estimated position lies about 2 km from the arc, and Godfrey's WSPR point about 43 NM outside it (published: about 40 NM).
  • ATSB 2014–17: measured coverage from Geoscience Australia's four sonar mosaics (Fugro deep tow, Fugro AUV, GO Phoenix, Dong Hai Jiu 101), on a grid of about 2 km. Total about 124,000 km², about 3% more than the official 120,000 km² because the grid slightly widens swath edges. The darker the shade, the more likely it is AUV coverage of difficult terrain.
  • OI 2018: the outer boundary published by CAPTION, minus the 2014–17 area: about 125,500 km². Ocean Infinity reports 112,000 km² searched, so about 13,000 km² may have been missed. The likely gaps are estimated from the ship's track: the AUVs worked far from the ship, so cells the ship never came within 25 km of are marked. They lie mostly inside the arc, at 29–32°S and 25–27°S. This is a weak estimate.
  • OI 2025–26: the dashed line is the area proposed in 2024 (about 15,800 km², CAPTION). The fill is an estimate: a 4.2 km band around the ship's hourly positions, set so that the Dec 2025 – Jan 2026 phase matches the reported ~7,200 km². The March 2025 track (lighter) was partly a survey before the contract was signed; by the same method about 4,200 km², of which about 3,500 km² does not overlap the later phase. Check: this leaves about 8,100 km² of the proposed area unsearched (the whole inner, north-west sub-area and about 1,800 km² of the south-east one), close to the officially remaining 7,429 km².
  • Debris positions: the official list gives only beach names; coordinates were assigned by hand at beach or town level (about 5–30 km).
  • Posterior layer: the pre-registered analysis; pick the primary run or any of the 13 sensitivity runs in the layer panel. Its straight-edged blocks are real features of the inputs, not drawing errors: the drift prior and the piloted-glide model are uniform bands, so the probability changes in steps at 28°S, 33°S, 36°S and at 25 NM and 100 NM from the arc.
  • Seafloor feature names (Broken Ridge, Diamantina Fracture Zone, Naturaliste Plateau, Ninety East Ridge) are placed approximately, for orientation only.

The research gap

There is no single public, updated estimate of where probability remains after three unsuccessful searches. For Air France 447, a Bayesian re-analysis of this kind is what led to the wreck.
